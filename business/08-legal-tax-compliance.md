# 08 · Legal, Tax & Compliance Notes

> General notes gathered during planning — **not legal or tax advice**. Confirm everything with a Portuguese contabilista (certified accountant).

## Portugal — tax set-up (founder is a sole trader)
- Recibos verdes / fatura-recibo can be used for selling goods, but the activity needs a **CAE for online retail** added at Finanças.
- **Art. 53 VAT exemption** (turnover ≤ €15,000) — may not apply/may not suit if importing or doing intra-EU trade; check.
- **EU distance-sales threshold €10,000** per year across the EU → above that, charge destination-country VAT via **OSS**.
- Many EU wholesalers (Van Os, Drytech) require a **valid EU VAT number (VIES)** → decide with contabilista.

## Product compliance
- **First aid kits:** EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR) / CE. Prefer suppliers with an EU Declaration of Conformity (Risen Medical has one for RSF-001, Class I non-sterile). US later: FDA product code OHO.
- **Lithium batteries** (radios, power banks, torches): air shipping restrictions from China; prefer EU stock or battery-free versions.
- **Food:** labels in Portuguese/Spanish (allergens, nutrition), remaining shelf life at dispatch; buy only from EU producers.
- **Water filters:** only quote certifications/lab results the supplier can document.
- **Consumer law (EU):** 14-day right of withdrawal, 2-year legal guarantee, clear shipping/returns info, GDPR privacy policy, cookie consent.

## Advertising claims
- Avoid fear-based or unverifiable claims ("survive any disaster"). Quote shelf lives, filter capacities and certifications exactly as the supplier documents them.
