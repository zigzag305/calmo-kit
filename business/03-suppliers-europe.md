## Questions we ask every supplier
1. Do you dropship to end customers in PT/ES/EU? Blind shipping (no prices, our name on slip)? Per-order fee?
2. Reseller price vs retail (discount %)? Minimum advertised price policy?
3. Minimum first order and reorder? Payment terms?
4. Delivery time and cost to PT/ES per parcel, and for heavy items (buckets, multi-month packs)?
5. Returns, damaged or lost parcels — who pays, what process?
6. Product photos, descriptions, CSV/API feed or Shopify integration with stock sync?
7. What do you need to approve us? Do you accept a Portuguese sole trader (trabalhador independente) with NIF/VAT?
8. Food: manufacture date and remaining shelf life at dispatch? Labels in PT/ES (allergens, nutrition)?
9. Food: can you ship food to every EU country we sell to?
10. Water: certifications or lab reports we can quote in ads? Margin on replacement filters?
11. Kits: white label (our logo)? Minimum quantity and price?
