# 01 · Business Overview — CalmoKit

> Last updated: 22 Nov 2026 · Owner: Matias Ibañez (founder) · Status: pre-launch, supplier outreach in progress

## One-line pitch
CalmoKit is a premium emergency-preparedness e-commerce brand: ready-to-use 72-hour emergency kits, long-shelf-life food and water filtration, sold online in **Portugal and Spain first**, then the rest of the EU, then the US.

## Why now
- The EU now officially recommends every household keeps a **72-hour emergency kit** (EU Preparedness Union Strategy, 2025).
- Spain's national blackout (April 2025) and repeated floods (DANA) pushed demand for radios, torches, power banks and 72h kits in Spain and Portugal.
- Most local offers are either cheap generic kits or expensive niche "prepper" shops. Gap: a **calm, premium, well-designed** brand for normal families ("calm", not "doom").

## What we sell (product pillars)
1. **72-hour emergency kits (hero product)** — backpack for 1, 2 or 4 people: water + filtration, food rations, first aid, light, radio, thermal blankets, power.
2. **Long-shelf-life food** — freeze-dried meals and "food buckets" (10–25 year shelf life) for 1 week up to 6 months.
3. **Water filtration** — personal filters/straws, gravity filters, jerrycans (LifeSaver, Katadyn, LifeStraw tier).
4. **Add-ons / order bumps** — crank/solar radios, torches, first aid kits, thermal blankets, power banks.
5. **Later** — own private-label CalmoKit kit made by a Chinese OEM (logo on bag and box), stocked in an EU warehouse.

Current website catalogue (see `src/data/products.ts`): 72H Crisis Pack, Wilderness Field Kit, Home Blackout & Power Kit, Food & Hydration Case + individual gear (water filter, trauma kit, weather radio, freeze-dried ration trio). Prices on the site are placeholders until supplier costs are confirmed.

## Target customer
- Families and couples in Portugal and Spain (25–55), homeowners, parents, people in flood/earthquake/blackout-prone areas.
- Secondary: small companies and offices (first aid / emergency kits for staff), gift buyers.
- Languages needed: **Portuguese and Spanish** (English as fallback).

## Markets & sequence
1. **Portugal + Spain** (home market, founder based in Lisbon area).
2. Rest of EU (via the same EU suppliers / EU warehouse).
3. **US** later (US dropship suppliers already researched: My Patriot Supply, ReadyWise, Wise, etc. — not active).

## Business model
- **Phase 1 — Dropshipping (start with ~€0 stock):** sell supplier products; supplier ships to the customer. Validate demand and ad performance.
- **Phase 2 — Wholesale:** once volume is proven, buy stock from the best EU suppliers for better margin and faster delivery.
- **Phase 3 — Private label:** own-brand CalmoKit 72h kit from a Chinese OEM (e.g. Risen Medical, MOQ 100 sets, logo on bag/box), shipped by sea into an EU warehouse (CJ Germany or a Portuguese 3PL) for 2–4 day delivery.
- Fallback when a supplier won't dropship: "buy after the customer pays" (order the item from the supplier only after receiving payment).

## Positioning vs competitors
- Local PT/ES competitors seen: Kits de Emergência (PT), Safe Kit (PT), EmerPlan (ES), Tactical Store 911 (ES), Liofilizado & Co (FR group, ES/PT shops). Typical 72h kit retail: €70 (basic) to €250–400 (premium).
- CalmoKit edge: premium design + bilingual PT/ES + strong paid marketing (Cuyo) + curated "calm" brand + bundles by situation (blackout, flood, evacuation, car).
