# 06 · Supplier Outreach Playbook

## Rules (from founder feedback)
- Lead with **value for the supplier**, not with "we are new".
- Position CalmoKit as e-commerce + digital marketing experts, backed by **Cuyo Marketing** (founder's agency).
- Ask for **dropship as Phase 1**, wholesale as Phase 2. Sound decisive — never "or we could just be a reseller if you prefer".
- **Never** write "launching soon" or "you are on our shortlist".
- Under ~140 words, **plain text**, no links, no attachments, one low-friction question at the end.
- Do not write `calmokit.eu` (or any domain) as text — Gmail auto-links it. Brand names like "MILITARY.EU" also auto-link → use "Military B2B team".
- Send from `matias@calmokit.eu`, signed "Matias Ibañez, Founder, CalmoKit".

## Deliverability (new domain)
- Max ~3 cold emails per day, spaced 40–70 minutes apart, business hours of the recipient.
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC are set and verified for `calmokit.eu`.
- Reply quickly to every answer (replies build sender reputation).
