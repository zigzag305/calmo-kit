# Calmo Kit (`www.calmokit.com`) — SEO, AEO/GEO & Launch Marketing Playbook

Built using the frameworks from `open-seo` and `digital-marketing-pro`.

---

## 1. Technical SEO & AEO/GEO Already Implemented
- **Structured Data (`index.html`)**: `Organization`, `WebSite`, `ItemList` of `Product` schemas (prices, stock, ratings), and `FAQPage` JSON-LD for Google Rich Results & AI Answer Engines.
- **AI Search / GEO (`public/llms.txt`)**: Clean machine-readable catalog & pricing summary for ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Mode.
- **Crawl & Indexation**: `public/robots.txt` + `public/sitemap.xml` + canonical tags + `hreflang` (`en` / `es`) + full OpenGraph & Twitter Cards.
